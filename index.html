I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Clemson University, working in the IS-WiN Laboratory with Dr. Fatemeh Afghah. My research focuses on machine learning and AI for wireless communication systems, including federated learning, privacy-preserving AI, and LLM-assisted intelligence for ORAN and 6G networks.

I completed my MSE in Electrical Engineering at The University of Alabama in Huntsville, where I worked with Dr. Dinh C. Nguyen in the NIS Lab on federated learning, quantum computing, and secure distributed systems.
