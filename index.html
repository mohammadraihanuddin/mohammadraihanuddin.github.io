Mohammad Raihan Uddin

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Clemson University, working in the IS-WiN Laboratory with Dr. Fatemeh Afghah. My research focuses on machine learning and AI for wireless communication systems, including federated learning, AI safety, foundation models, and LLM-assisted intelligence for 5G and 6G networks.

I completed my MSE in Electrical Engineering at The University of Alabama in Huntsville, where I worked with Dr. Dinh C. Nguyen in the NIS Lab on federated learning, quantum computing, and secure distributed systems.

Research

I am broadly interested in machine learning, artificial intelligence, AI safety, foundation models, and wireless communications. I am particularly excited about building foundation models and trustworthy AI systems for next-generation wireless communication and 5G/6G infrastructures.
