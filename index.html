Mohammad Raihan Uddin

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Clemson University, working in the IS-WiN Laboratory with Dr. Fatemeh Afghah. My research focuses on AI for next-generation wireless systems, including wireless foundation models, Open RAN security, integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), and trustworthy machine learning for 5G and 6G networks.

I completed my MSE in Electrical Engineering at The University of Alabama in Huntsville, where I worked with Dr. Dinh C. Nguyen in the NIS Lab on federated learning, quantum computing, and secure distributed systems.

Research

I build trustworthy AI for 5G/6G networks. At Clemson, I work on defending deep reinforcement learning xApps in Open RAN against backdoor attacks (ORAN-DEFEND), parameter-efficient adaptation of large wireless foundation models (RFPrompt), and mixture-of-experts models for sensing-assisted communications (ISACMoE). Previously at UAH, I worked on quantum federated learning, secure UAV networks with zero-knowledge proofs, and attack detection for smart grids.
